## Kontho PDF Reader 0.7.2

Voice settings in one place, a proper Appearance screen with colours, and pop-ups that look like Kontho.

### Reading voice
- All **Read aloud settings** now live in **Settings → Reading voice**: reading speed, voice pitch, continue to next page, keep reading in the background, show position, skip page numbers, skip repeated book title, and **Choose Bangla voice** (device voice).
- The **settings button** in the PDF page's three-dot menu now opens this screen.
- **Narration pace** was removed from Settings: it was a second, coarser copy of Reading speed. Your saved speed is kept.
- **Scan the next page ahead** and **Keep OCR text until the book is deleted** moved to **Settings → OCR**, next to the cache limit.

### Azure voice, step by step
- A new **How to get your free Speech key** guide inside the Azure voice card: sign in, find Speech services, fill the form (Free F0), open Keys and Endpoint, copy the key and region, paste them here.
- **Open Azure portal**, **Paste key** and **Paste region** buttons. The region is cleaned up for you (lower case, no spaces).

### Appearance
- New **Settings → Appearance** screen with **Theme**, **Colour**, **Library layout** and **Grid columns**.
- **Colour** (Green, Blue, Teal, Purple, Rose, Orange) shows when the light theme is in use.
- **Grid columns** is hidden when the layout is **List**.
- Library layout and Grid columns are no longer separate rows in Settings.

### Pop-ups
- Language, sort, about, changelog, update, privacy and cache-limit pop-ups now use Kontho's own rounded style and colours instead of the phone's default alert box.
