## Kontho PDF Reader 0.8.0

- **New formats:** open **EPUB** books and **CBZ / CBR** comics and manga, from the library, the file picker or other apps.
- **Comics:** read left to right, right to left (manga) or as a vertical strip (webtoon). Pinch or double-tap to zoom; tap the sides to turn pages.
- **EPUB:** chapter list, text size, and light, sepia or dark reading colours. Your place is remembered.
- Read aloud is for PDFs only for now. CBR files made with RAR 5 are not supported yet.
